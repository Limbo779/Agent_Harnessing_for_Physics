### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

* **Flagged Step:**


$$k_x = \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar} \quad \text{[cite: 4]}$$


* **Nature of Error:**
Algebraic and Dimensional Error in the intermediate expression for $k_x$ in terms of $E_x$.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. **Algebraic Proof:**
Starting from the decoupled horizontal ODE:

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} = E_x X(x) \quad \text{[cite: 1]}$$



Multiply both sides by $-\frac{2m}{\hbar^2}$:

$$\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar^2} X(x) = 0$$



Comparing this to the standard 1D Helmholtz equation $\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + k_x^2 X(x) = 0$ identifies:

$$k_x^2 = \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar^2} \implies k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2mE_x}}{\hbar}$$



Agent 1 omitted the square root sign over the quantity $(2mE_x)$ in the middle step.


2. **Dimensional Proof:**
Checking the dimensions of Agent 1's intermediate formula $\frac{2mE_x}{\hbar}$:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2mE_x}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M}\cdot\text{L}^2\cdot\text{T}^{-2})}{\text{M}\cdot\text{L}^2\cdot\text{T}^{-1}} = \text{M}\cdot\text{T}^{-1} \quad (\text{units of kg}\cdot\text{s}^{-1})$$



This does not equal wavenumber dimensions ($\text{L}^{-1}$).
Checking the correct expression $\frac{\sqrt{2mE_x}}{\hbar}$:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{\sqrt{2mE_x}}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\sqrt{\text{M}^2\cdot\text{L}^2\cdot\text{T}^{-2}}}{\text{M}\cdot\text{L}^2\cdot\text{T}^{-1}} = \frac{\text{M}\cdot\text{L}\cdot\text{T}^{-1}}{\text{M}\cdot\text{L}^2\cdot\text{T}^{-1}} = \text{L}^{-1} \quad (\text{units of m}^{-1})$$


3. **Consistency Proof:**
Substituting $E_x = \frac{1}{2}m u^2 \cos^2\theta$ into Agent 1's intermediate expression gives:



$$\frac{2m \left(\frac{1}{2} m u^2 \cos^2\theta\right)}{\hbar} = \frac{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}{\hbar} \neq \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$



Whereas substituting $E_x$ into the square-rooted expression gives:

$$\frac{\sqrt{2m \left(\frac{1}{2} m u^2 \cos^2\theta\right)}}{\hbar} = \frac{\sqrt{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2mE_x}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$
