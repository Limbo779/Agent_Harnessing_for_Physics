### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### Flaw 1: Missing Square Root in Normalization Constant $C_y$ & False Equality

* **Flagged Step:**
> *"$C_y=\frac{1}{l_g[Ai^{\prime2}(z_0)-z_0Ai^2(z_0)]}=\frac{(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2})^{1/6}}{Ai^{\prime2}(z_0)-z_0Ai^2(z_0)}$"* and *"$C_y=\frac{1}{l_g\vert{}Ai^\prime(z_0)\vert{}}=\frac{(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2})^{1/6}}{\vert{}Ai^\prime(z_0)\vert{}}$"*
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic, Dimensional, and False Equality.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. From the normalization integral $C_y^2 l_g \left[ Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0) \right] = 1$, taking the square root of both sides yields:



$$C_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g} \sqrt{Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0)}}$$



Agent 5 omitted the square root operation $\sqrt{\dots}$ on both $l_g$ and the bracketed Airy expression in the denominator.


2. Because $l_g = \left(\frac{\hbar^2}{2m^2g}\right)^{1/3}$, we have:



$$\frac{1}{l_g} = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}, \quad \text{whereas} \quad \frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g}} = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}$$



Agent 5 asserted that $\frac{1}{l_g} = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}$, which is algebraically false.


3. **Dimensional Analysis Check:**
Since $\int_0^\infty \vert{}Y(y)\vert{}^2 dy = 1$, the 1D wavefunction component must have dimension $[Y(y)] = \text{L}^{-1/2}$. Because $Ai(z)$ and $z_0$ are unitless ($\text{Dim}[z_0] = 1$), $C_y$ must have dimension $\text{L}^{-1/2}$.



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g}}\right] = \text{L}^{-1/2} \quad \text{(Correct)}, \quad \text{whereas} \quad \text{Dim}\left[\frac{1}{l_g}\right] = \text{L}^{-1} \quad \text{(Incorrect)}$$




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$C_y = \frac{\left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}}{\sqrt{Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0)}}$$



For quantized boundary-enforced states ($Ai(z_0) = 0$):



$$C_y = \frac{\left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}}{\vert{}Ai'(z_0)\vert{}}$$
