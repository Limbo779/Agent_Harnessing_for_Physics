### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### **Flaw 1: Incorrect Prefactors in Airy Asymptotic Expansions**

* **Flagged Step:**
> "$\text{Ai}(z)\sim\frac{1}{2~\pi z^{1/4}}\exp(-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2})\xrightarrow{z\rightarrow+\infty}0$"
> "$\text{Bi}(z)\sim\frac{1}{\pi z^{1/4}}\exp(\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2})\xrightarrow{z\rightarrow+\infty}\infty$"
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic / Asymptotic Constant Error.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
Evaluating the asymptotic behavior of the integral representation of Airy functions via the method of steepest descent / stationary phase for $z \gg 0$:

$$\text{Ai}(z) = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{0}^{\infty} \cos\left(\frac{t^3}{3} + z t\right) dt \implies \text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right) \quad \text{as } z \to +\infty$$


$$\text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right) \quad \text{as } z \to +\infty$$



Agent 3 omitted the square root on $\pi$ in both prefactors, writing $\pi$ instead of $\sqrt{\pi}$.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$\text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{1}{2 \sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right) \quad \text{as } z \to +\infty$$


$$\text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right) \quad \text{as } z \to +\infty$$

* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
The system potential is defined with an impenetrable barrier at $y < 0$ ($V(y) = \infty$ for $y < 0$). Wavefunction continuity at an infinite potential wall requires:



$$Y(0) = 0$$

* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$Y(y) = C_y \text{Ai}(z(y)) \quad \text{where } Y(0) = 0 \implies \text{Ai}(z(0)) = 0 \quad \text{with } z(0) = -\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} \frac{E_y}{mg}$$
