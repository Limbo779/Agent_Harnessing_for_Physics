### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### **Flaw 1: Flawed Definition of Horizontal Wavenumber $k_x$ and Invalid Algebraic Step**

* **Flagged Step:**
> "`where $k_{x}=\frac{2mE_{x}}{\hbar}=\frac{mu~cos~\theta}{\hbar}$`"
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Definition, Dimensional, and Algebraic Fudge.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. *Definition & Dimensional Proof:* From the horizontal ODE $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} = E_x X(x)$, dividing by $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}$ gives:

$$\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar^2} X(x) = 0$$



Comparing this to the canonical Helmholtz wave equation $\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + k_x^2 X(x) = 0$ establishes:

$$k_x^2 = \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar^2} \implies k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar}$$



Evaluating dimensions for Agent 1's definition ($k_x = \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar}$):



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2m E_x}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2})}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{M T}^{-1} \neq \text{L}^{-1}$$



Evaluating dimensions for the correct definition:

$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\sqrt{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2})}}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \frac{\text{M L T}^{-1}}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{L}^{-1}$$


2. *Algebraic Fudge Proof:* Substituting $E_x = \frac{1}{2} m u^2 \cos^2\theta$ into Agent 1's expression gives:

$$\frac{2m E_x}{\hbar} = \frac{2m \left(\frac{1}{2} m u^2 \cos^2\theta\right)}{\hbar} = \frac{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}{\hbar}$$



Agent 1 set $\frac{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$. Dividing both sides by $\frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$ implies $m u \cos\theta = 1$, which is mathematically invalid for general physical constants $(m, u, \theta)$.




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar} = \frac{\sqrt{2m \left(\frac{1}{2} m u^2 \cos^2\theta\right)}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$
