### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### **Flaw 1: Omission of Square Root in Vertical Normalization Constant $C_y$ (General Case)**

* **Flagged Step:**
> $C_y = \frac{1}{l_g[Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0)]} = \frac{(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2})^{1/6}}{Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0)}$
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
Starting from the normalized probability density equation:

$$\int_0^\infty \vert{}Y(y)\vert{}^2 dy = 1 \implies C_y^2 l_g \int_{z_0}^\infty Ai^2(z) dz = 1$$



Substituting the definite integral result $\int_{z_0}^\infty Ai^2(z) dz = Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0)$:

$$C_y^2 l_g \left[ Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0) \right] = 1$$



Solving for $C_y$ requires taking the square root of both sides:

$$C_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g}} \frac{1}{\sqrt{Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0)}}$$



Since $l_g = \left(\frac{\hbar^2}{2m^2 g}\right)^{1/3}$, we have $l_g^{-1/2} = \left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}$. Agent 5 correctly evaluated $l_g^{-1/2}$, but omitted the square root on the bracket term $\left[ Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0) \right]$ in the denominator.


An un-squared bracket in $C_y$ leads to an unnormalized wave function where $\int_0^\infty \vert{}Y(y)\vert{}^2 dy \neq 1$.
* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$C_y = \frac{\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}}{\sqrt{Ai'^2(z_0) - z_0 Ai^2(z_0)}} = \frac{\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}}{\sqrt{Ai'^2(z_0) + \left(\frac{m u^3 \sin^3\theta}{2g\hbar}\right)^{2/3} Ai^2(z_0)}}$$

#### **Flaw 2: Omission of Square Roots in Horizontal Normalization Factors $C_x$**

* **Flagged Step:**
> `$C_x = \frac{1}{L}$ for spatial box normalization of length L, or $C_x = \frac{1}{2\pi\hbar}$ for continuous momentum delta-normalization`
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. For spatial box normalization over length $L$:

$$\int_0^L \vert{}X(x)\vert{}^2 dx = \vert{}C_x\vert{}^2 \int_0^L dx = \vert{}C_x\vert{}^2 L = 1 \implies \vert{}C_x\vert{}^2 = \frac{1}{L} \implies C_x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}}$$



Agent 5 wrote $C_x = \frac{1}{L}$, resulting in $[C_x] = \text{L}^{-1}$ instead of the required $\text{L}^{-1/2}$.


2. For momentum Dirac-delta normalization in $p$-space:

$$\int_{-\infty}^\infty X_{p'}^*(x) X_p(x) dx = \vert{}C_x\vert{}^2 \int_{-\infty}^\infty e^{\frac{i}{\hbar}(p - p')x} dx = \vert{}C_x\vert{}^2 \cdot 2\pi\hbar \, \delta(p - p') = \delta(p - p')$$


$$\vert{}C_x\vert{}^2 \cdot 2\pi\hbar = 1 \implies C_x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}$$



Agent 5 wrote $C_x = \frac{1}{2\pi\hbar}$, omitting the square root.




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$C_x = \begin{cases} \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}}, & \text{for Box Normalization over length } L \\ \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}, & \text{for Momentum Dirac-Delta Normalization} \end{cases}$$
