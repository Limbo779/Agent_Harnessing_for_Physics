### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

* **Flagged Step 1:** Derivation of $C_y$ in Section 2 ($C_y^2 l_g [\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)] = 1 \implies C_y = \frac{1}{l_g [\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)]}$).


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional Error.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
Starting from the normalization condition $C_y^2 l_g \left[ \text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0) \right] = 1$:



$$C_y^2 = \frac{1}{l_g \left[ \text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0) \right]}$$



Taking the principal square root of both sides gives:

$$C_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g \left[ \text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0) \right]}} = \frac{l_g^{-1/2}}{\sqrt{\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)}}$$



Agent 5 omitted the square root operation over both $l_g$ and the bracketed expression $\left[ \text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0) \right]$.


Dimensional analysis check:



$$\int_0^\infty \vert{}Y(y)\vert{}^2 dy = 1 \implies [Y(y)] = \text{L}^{-1/2}$$



Since $\text{Ai}(z)$ and $z_0$ are dimensionless, $[C_y]$ must equal $\text{L}^{-1/2}$.
Because $[l_g] = \text{L}$, setting $C_y \propto \frac{1}{l_g}$ yields $[C_y] = \text{L}^{-1}$, which violates the 1D spatial normalization requirement.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$C_y = \frac{\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}}{\sqrt{\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)}}$$

* **Flagged Step 2:** Boundary-enforced simplification in Section 2 ($C_y = \frac{1}{l_g \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}} = \frac{(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2})^{1/6}}{\vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}$).


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic Inconsistency.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
When $\text{Ai}(z_0) = 0$ ($z_0 = a_n$), the bracketed term reduces to $\text{Ai}'^2(a_n)$.


Agent 5 writes the intermediate expression as $\frac{1}{l_g \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}$ while writing the final expression as $\frac{(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2})^{1/6}}{\vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}$.


Since $l_g = \left(\frac{\hbar^2}{2m^2 g}\right)^{1/3}$, we have $\frac{1}{l_g} = \left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} \neq \left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}$.


The power $1/6$ arises strictly from $\frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g}} = l_g^{-1/2} = \left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}$. Thus, writing $\frac{1}{l_g}$ in the intermediate step contradicts the final line.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$C_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g} \vert{}\text{Ai}'(a_n)\vert{}} = \frac{\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}}{\vert{}\text{Ai}'(a_n)\vert{}}$$
