### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

* **Flagged Step 1:**


$$C_y = \frac{1}{l_g [\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)]} = \frac{(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2})^{1/6}}{\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)} \quad \text{and} \quad C_y = \frac{1}{l_g \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}} = \frac{(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2})^{1/6}}{\vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}} \quad \text{[cite: 11]}$$


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional Error in solving for $C_y$ from $C_y^2$.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. **Algebraic Proof:**
Starting from the correct normalization equation:



$$C_y^2 l_g [\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)] = 1 \quad \text{[cite: 11]}$$



Dividing by $l_g [\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)]$:

$$C_y^2 = \frac{1}{l_g [\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)]}$$



Taking the square root of both sides:

$$C_y = \frac{1}{\sqrt{l_g} \sqrt{\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)}} = \frac{l_g^{-1/2}}{\sqrt{\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)}}$$



Agent 5 omitted the square root exponent ($1/2$) on the denominator terms on the left-hand side of those equations ($\frac{1}{l_g [\dots]}$ and $\frac{1}{l_g \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}$), while simultaneously evaluating $l_g^{-1/2} = \left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}$ in the numerator on the right-hand side.


2. **Dimensional Proof:**
For 1D spatial normalization $\int_0^\infty \vert{}Y(y)\vert{}^2 dy = 1$, the dimension of $Y(y)$ and $C_y$ must be $[C_y] = \text{L}^{-1/2}$.
Checking dimensions of Agent 5's expression $\frac{1}{l_g \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}$:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{1}{l_g \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}\right] = \text{L}^{-1} \neq \text{L}^{-1/2} \quad \text{[cite: 13]}$$



Checking the corrected expression $\frac{1}{l_g^{1/2} \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}$:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{1}{l_g^{1/2} \vert{}\text{Ai}'(z_0)\vert{}}\right] = \text{L}^{-1/2} \quad \text{[cite: 13]}$$




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$C_y = \frac{1}{l_g^{1/2} \sqrt{\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)}} = \frac{\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/6}}{\sqrt{\text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)}}$$



For boundary-enforced states where $\text{Ai}(z_0) = 0$:
