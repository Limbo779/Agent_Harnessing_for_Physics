$$\text{Dim}\left[\left(\frac{\hbar^2 g^2}{2m}\right)^{1/3}\right] = \text{M}^{1/3} \text{L}^2 \text{T}^{-2} \neq \text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2} \quad (\text{Energy})$$
