### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### **Flaw 1: Flawed Definition of Wavenumber $k_x$ and Invalid Algebraic Step**

* **Flagged Step:**
> "$k_{x}\equiv\frac{2mE_{x}}{\hbar}$ is the horizontal wavenumber."
> "$k_{x}=\frac{2m(\frac{1}{2}mu^{2}cos^{2}\theta)}{\hbar}=\frac{m^{2}u^{2}cos^{2}\theta}{\hbar}=\frac{mu~cos~\theta}{\hbar}$"
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Definition, Dimensional, and Algebraic Fudge.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. *Definition & Dimensional Proof:* Substituting $X(x) = e^{i k_x x}$ into $\frac{d^2 X}{dx^2} + k_x^2 X = 0$ yields:

$$-k_x^2 X(x) + \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar^2} X(x) = 0 \implies k_x^2 = \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar^2} \implies k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar}$$



Evaluating dimensions for Agent 2's definition:

$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2m E_x}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2})}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{M T}^{-1} \neq \text{L}^{-1}$$



Evaluating dimensions for the correct definition:

$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\sqrt{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2})}}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \frac{\text{M L T}^{-1}}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{L}^{-1}$$


2. *Algebraic Fudge Proof:* In Step 3, Agent 2 wrote $\frac{m^{2}u^{2}\cos^{2}\theta}{\hbar} = \frac{mu\cos\theta}{\hbar}$. Dividing both sides by $\frac{mu\cos\theta}{\hbar}$ implies $m u \cos\theta = 1$, which is mathematically untrue for arbitrary physical constants $(m, u, \theta)$.




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar} = \frac{\sqrt{2m \left(\frac{1}{2} m u^2 \cos^2\theta\right)}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$

#### **Flaw 2: Missing Square Root in Dirac Delta Normalization Constant**

* **Flagged Step:**
> "$\vert{}A\vert{}^{2}\cdot2\pi\delta(k_{x}-k_{x}^{\prime})=\delta(k_{x}-k_{x}^{\prime}) \implies \vert{}A\vert{}=\frac{1}{2\pi}\Rightarrow X(x)=\frac{1}{2\pi}e^{ik_{x}x}$"
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
Isolating $\vert{}A\vert{}^2$ from $\vert{}A\vert{}^2 \cdot 2\pi = 1$:

$$\vert{}A\vert{}^2 = \frac{1}{2\pi} \implies \vert{}A\vert{} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{2\pi}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$$



Agent 2 omitted the square root operation when solving for $\vert{}A\vert{}$.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{i k_x x}$$

#### **Flaw 3: Missing Square Root and Dimensional Failure in Box Normalization Constant**

* **Flagged Step:**
> "$\vert{}A\vert{}^{2}L=1\Rightarrow A=\frac{1}{L}\Rightarrow X(x)=\frac{1}{L}e^{i(\frac{mu~cos~\theta}{\hbar})x}$"
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. *Algebraic Proof:* From $\vert{}A\vert{}^2 L = 1$, dividing by $L$ gives $\vert{}A\vert{}^2 = \frac{1}{L}$. Taking the square root yields:

$$A = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}}$$


2. *Dimensional Proof:* For a 1D spatial wave function, $\int_0^L \vert{}X(x)\vert{}^2 dx = 1$ dictates:

$$\text{Dim}[X(x)] = \text{L}^{-1/2}$$



Setting $A = \frac{1}{L}$ produces $\text{Dim}[X(x)] = \text{L}^{-1}$, which violates standard 1D spatial wave function dimensions.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}} e^{i \left( \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar} \right) x}$$
