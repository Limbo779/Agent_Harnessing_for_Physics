### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

* **Flagged Step:** Asymptotic expansions in Step 4:



$$\text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{1}{2 \pi z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right), \quad \text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{1}{\pi z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right)$$


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic / Mathematical (Incorrect Constant Prefactors in Asymptotic Formulae).


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
By standard asymptotic evaluation of Airy functions via the method of steepest descent (e.g., Abramowitz & Stegun 10.4.59 and 10.4.63), the leading asymptotic behaviors for $z \gg 0$ ($z \to +\infty$) are given by:

$$\text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right)$$


$$\text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right)$$



Agent 3 wrote $\pi$ instead of $\sqrt{\pi}$ in the denominator prefactor for both functions ($\frac{1}{2\pi z^{1/4}}$ and $\frac{1}{\pi z^{1/4}}$).


While this typographical prefactor error does not alter the physical qualitative behavior ($\lim_{z \to \infty} \text{Bi}(z) = \infty$, enforcing $C_2 = 0$), the formula as stated is mathematically inaccurate.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$\text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right) \xrightarrow{z \to +\infty} 0$$


$$\text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3} z^{3/2}\right) \xrightarrow{z \to +\infty} \infty$$
