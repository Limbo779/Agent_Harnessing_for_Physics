### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

* **Flagged Step 1:** Definition of $k_x$ in Section 1 ($k_x \equiv \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar}$) and its subsequent substitution in Section 3 ($k_x = \frac{2m(\frac{1}{2}m u^2 \cos^2\theta)}{\hbar} = \frac{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$).


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional Error.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
Rearranging $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} \frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} = E_x X(x)$ into standard Helmholtz form yields:



$$\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar^2} X(x) = 0$$



Comparing this to $\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + k_x^2 X(x) = 0$ requires $k_x^2 = \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar^2}$, which gives:



$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar}$$



Agent 2 omitted the square root over $2m E_x$ and omitted the exponent on $\hbar$ in the definition. Performing dimensional analysis:



$$\text{Dim}[k_x] = \text{L}^{-1}$$


$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2m E_x}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2})}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{M T}^{-1} \neq \text{L}^{-1}$$


$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\sqrt{\text{M}^2 \text{L}^2 \text{T}^{-2}}}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \frac{\text{M L T}^{-1}}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{L}^{-1}$$



In Section 3, Agent 2 wrote $\frac{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$, which took an unstated square root of the numerator while ignoring the denominator, masking the initial definition error.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar} = \frac{\sqrt{2m \left( \frac{1}{2} m u^2 \cos^2\theta \right)}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$



---

* **Flagged Step 2:** Dirac Delta Normalization Constant derivation in Section 4 ($\vert{}A\vert{}^2 \cdot 2\pi \delta(k_x - k_x') = \delta(k_x - k_x') \implies \vert{}A\vert{} = \frac{1}{2\pi}$).


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic Error.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} X_{k_x'}^*(x) X_{k_x}(x) dx = \vert{}A\vert{}^2 \cdot 2\pi \delta(k_x - k_x') = \delta(k_x - k_x')$$



Dividing both sides by $2\pi \delta(k_x - k_x')$ yields:

$$\vert{}A\vert{}^2 = \frac{1}{2\pi} \implies \vert{}A\vert{} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$$



Agent 2 omitted the square root when solving for $\vert{}A\vert{}$.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$\vert{}A\vert{} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} \implies X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{i k_x x}$$

* **Flagged Step 3:** Box Normalization Constant derivation in Section 4 ($\vert{}A\vert{}^2 L = 1 \implies A = \frac{1}{L}$).


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional Error.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
$$\int_0^L \vert{}X(x)\vert{}^2 dx = \vert{}A\vert{}^2 \int_0^L dx = \vert{}A\vert{}^2 L = 1 \implies \vert{}A\vert{}^2 = \frac{1}{L} \implies A = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}}$$



Dimensional check: 1D wave function normalization requires $\int_0^L \vert{}X(x)\vert{}^2 dx = 1$, meaning $[X(x)] = \text{L}^{-1/2}$. Setting $A = 1/L$ gives $[X(x)] = \text{L}^{-1}$, which violates 1D spatial wave function dimensions and would cause the overall 2D spatial wave function $\psi(x, y) = X(x) Y(y)$ to have dimension $\text{L}^{-3/2}$ instead of the required $\text{L}^{-1}$.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$A = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}} \implies X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}} e^{i \left( \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar} \right) x}$$
