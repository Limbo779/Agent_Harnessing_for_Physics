### 2. Explicit Calculation of Normalization Constant $C_y$

Using the indefinite integral identity for Airy functions:

$$\int \text{Ai}^2(z) \, dz = z \text{Ai}^2(z) - \text{Ai}'^2(z)$$

Evaluating over the interval $[z_0, \infty)$:

$$\int_{z_0}^{\infty} \text{Ai}^2(z) \, dz = \left[ z \text{Ai}^2(z) - \text{Ai}'^2(z) \right]_{z_0}^{\infty} = 0 - \left( z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0) - \text{Ai}'^2(z_0) \right) = \text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0)$$

Substituting this result back into the normalization condition:

$$C_y^2 l_g \left[ \text{Ai}'^2(z_0) - z_0 \text{Ai}^2(z_0) \right] = 1$$

Taking the square root on both sides to solve explicitly for $C_y$:
