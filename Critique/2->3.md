### 1. Step-by-Step Mathematical Verification

* **Step 1: Decoupled Vertical ODE Setup**


* The vertical Schrödinger equation under uniform gravity $V(y) = mgy$ for $y \ge 0$ is correctly written as:

$$-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\frac{d^{2}Y(y)}{dy^{2}}+mgyY(y)=E_{y}Y(y) \quad \text{[cite: 5]}$$


* Factoring out $mg$ yields the correct form:

$$-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\frac{d^{2}Y(y)}{dy^{2}}+mg\left(y-\frac{E_{y}}{mg}\right)Y(y)=0 \quad \text{[cite: 5]}$$




* **Step 2: Dimensionless Variable Transformation & Derivative Scaling**


* The defined transformation variable is:

$$z(y)=\left(\frac{2m^{2}g}{\hbar^{2}}\right)^{1/3}\left(y-\frac{E_{y}}{mg}\right) \quad \text{[cite: 5]}$$


* **Dimensional Analysis Verification:**


$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right] = \frac{\text{M}^2 \cdot (\text{L}\cdot\text{T}^{-2})}{(\text{M}\cdot\text{L}^2\cdot\text{T}^{-1})^2} = \frac{\text{M}^2\cdot\text{L}\cdot\text{T}^{-2}}{\text{M}^2\cdot\text{L}^4\cdot\text{T}^{-2}} = \text{L}^{-3} \implies \text{Dim}\left[\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}\right] = \text{L}^{-1}$$



Since $\text{Dim}\left[y - \frac{E_y}{mg}\right] = \text{L}$, the product $\text{Dim}[z(y)] = \text{L}^{-1} \cdot \text{L} = 1$ is strictly unitless.


* The derivative transforms via the chain rule are correctly derived:

$$\frac{dz}{dy} = \alpha = \left(\frac{2m^{2}g}{\hbar^{2}}\right)^{1/3} \implies \frac{d^{2}Y}{dy^{2}} = \left(\frac{2m^{2}g}{\hbar^{2}}\right)^{2/3}\frac{d^{2}Y}{dz^{2}} \quad \text{[cite: 5]}$$




* **Step 3: Conversion to Airy's Canonical Equation**


* Substituting $z(y)$ and its second derivative into the vertical ODE gives:

$$-\frac{\hbar^{2}}{2m}\left(\frac{2m^{2}g}{\hbar^{2}}\right)^{2/3}\frac{d^{2}Y(z)}{dz^{2}}+\frac{mg}{\left(\frac{2m^{2}g}{\hbar^{2}}\right)^{1/3}}zY(z)=0 \quad \text{[cite: 5]}$$


* **First term coefficient verification:**

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\cdot \frac{2^{2/3} m^{4/3} g^{2/3}}{\hbar^{4/3}} = -\frac{1}{2^{1/3}} m^{1/3} g^{2/3} \hbar^{2/3} = -\left(\frac{mg^2\hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3} \quad \text{[cite: 6]}$$


* **Second term coefficient verification:**

$$\frac{mg}{\frac{2^{1/3} m^{2/3} g^{1/3}}{\hbar^{2/3}}} = \frac{m^{1/3} g^{2/3} \hbar^{2/3}}{2^{1/3}} = \left(\frac{mg^2\hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3} \quad \text{[cite: 6]}$$


* Dividing through by $-\left(\frac{mg^2\hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3}$ accurately produces Airy's canonical equation:

$$\frac{d^2Y(z)}{dz^2} - zY(z) = 0 \quad \text{[cite: 6]}$$




* **Step 4: Asymptotic Boundary Conditions & Solution Selection**

### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

$$\text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{1}{2 \pi z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right), \quad \text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{1}{\pi z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right) \quad \text{[cite: 6]}$$


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic / Typographical prefactor error in the standard asymptotic expansion formulas.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
The standard asymptotic expansions for Airy functions for large real positive arguments $z \gg 0$ (derived via the method of steepest descents / WKB approximation) are given by:

$$\text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{e^{-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}}}{2\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}}$$


$$\text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{e^{\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}}}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}}$$



Agent 3 wrote $\pi$ instead of $\sqrt{\pi}$ in the denominator prefactors of both expansions. While this does not alter the physical conclusion that $Bi(z) \to \infty$ and $Ai(z) \to 0$ as $z \to +\infty$, the explicit mathematical expressions presented are imprecise.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$\text{Ai}(z) \sim \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right) \xrightarrow{z \to +\infty} 0$$


$$\text{Bi}(z) \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right) \xrightarrow{z \to +\infty} \infty$$
