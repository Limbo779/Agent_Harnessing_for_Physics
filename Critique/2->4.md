### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

* **Flagged Step 1:**


$$z(0) = -\left(\frac{2mE_y^3}{\hbar^2 g^2}\right)^{1/3} \quad \text{[cite: 9]}$$


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional Error in moving $\frac{E_y}{mg}$ inside the cube root.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. **Algebraic Proof:**
Starting from Agent 4's expression at ground level $y=0$:



$$z(0) = \left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} \left(0 - \frac{E_y}{mg}\right) = -\left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} \frac{E_y}{mg} \quad \text{[cite: 9]}$$



To move $\frac{E_y}{mg}$ inside the cube root, cube the factor:

$$\left(\frac{E_y}{mg}\right)^3 = \frac{E_y^3}{m^3 g^3}$$



Multiplying inside the root:

$$\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2} \cdot \frac{E_y^3}{m^3 g^3} = \frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}$$



Taking the cube root gives:

$$z(0) = -\left(\frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}$$



Agent 4 mistakenly placed $m$ in the numerator ($\frac{2m E_y^3}{\hbar^2 g^2}$) instead of the denominator.


2. **Dimensional Proof:**
Checking dimensions of Agent 4's expression $\left(\frac{2m E_y^3}{\hbar^2 g^2}\right)^{1/3}$:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2m E_y^3}{\hbar^2 g^2}\right] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M}^3 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6})}{(\text{M}^2 \text{L}^4 \text{T}^{-2}) \cdot (\text{L}^2 \text{T}^{-4})} = \frac{\text{M}^4 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6}}{\text{M}^2 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6}} = \text{M}^2$$



Taking the cube root yields dimensions of $\text{M}^{2/3}$, which is not dimensionless ($\text{Dim}[z] \neq 1$).


Checking dimensions of the corrected expression $\left(\frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}$:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right] = \frac{\text{M}^3 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6}}{\text{M} \cdot (\text{L}^2 \text{T}^{-4}) \cdot (\text{M}^2 \text{L}^4 \text{T}^{-2})} = \frac{\text{M}^3 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6}}{\text{M}^3 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6}} = 1 \quad \text{[cite: 8]}$$




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$z(0) = -\left(\frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}$$
