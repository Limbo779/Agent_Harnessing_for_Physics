### 1. Step-by-Step Mathematical Verification

* **Step 1 (Decoupled Vertical ODE):** The vertical Schrödinger equation $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2Y(y)}{dy^2} + mgy Y(y) = E_y Y(y)$ and its rearrangement $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2Y(y)}{dy^2} + mg\left(y - \frac{E_y}{mg}\right)Y(y) = 0$ are exact.


* **Step 2 (Dimensionless Transformation):** The spatial transformation variable $z(y) = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}\left(y - \frac{E_y}{mg}\right)$ and substitution scale factor $\alpha = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}$ are correct. Dimensional evaluation confirms $\text{Dim}[\alpha] = \text{L}^{-1}$ and $\text{Dim}[z] = 1$ (strictly unitless). Applying the chain rule yields $\frac{d^2Y}{dy^2} = \alpha^2 \frac{d^2Y}{dz^2} = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{2/3} \frac{d^2Y}{dz^2}$ correctly.


* **Step 3 (Conversion to Airy's Canonical Equation):** The simplification of the first and second term coefficients, $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\alpha^2 = -\left(\frac{mg^2\hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3}$ and $\frac{mg}{\alpha} = \left(\frac{mg^2\hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3}$, is algebraically sound. Dividing by $-\left(\frac{mg^2\hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3}$ yields Airy's canonical ODE: $\frac{d^2Y(z)}{dz^2} - zY(z) = 0$.


* **Step 4 (Asymptotic Boundary Conditions & Solution):** Identifying that $Bi(z) \to \infty$ as $z \to +\infty$ and setting $C_2 = 0$ satisfies the asymptotic boundary condition $\lim_{y \to \infty} Y(y) = 0$. However, minor pre-factor errors exist in the stated asymptotic formulas, and the boundary condition at $y = 0$ was omitted.

### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### Flaw 1: Missing Square Root in Airy Asymptotic Expansion Pre-factors

* **Flagged Step:**
> *"$Ai(z)\sim\frac{1}{2~\pi z^{1/4}}exp(-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2})$"* and *"$Bi(z)\sim\frac{1}{\pi z^{1/4}}exp(\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2})$"*
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic / Asymptotic Pre-factor Error.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
Evaluating the integral representation $Ai(z) = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_0^\infty \cos\left(\frac{t^3}{3} + z t\right) dt$ via the method of steepest descent for $z \gg 0$ yields the standard leading-order asymptotic forms:

$$Ai(z) \sim \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right) \quad \text{as } z \to +\infty$$


$$Bi(z) \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right) \quad \text{as } z \to +\infty$$



Agent 3 omitted the square root on $\pi$ in both denominators ($\frac{1}{2\pi}$ instead of $\frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi}}$ and $\frac{1}{\pi}$ instead of $\frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi}}$).


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$Ai(z) \sim \frac{1}{2\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(-\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right), \quad Bi(z) \sim \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi} z^{1/4}} \exp\left(\frac{2}{3}z^{3/2}\right)$$

#### Flaw 2: Omission of Ground Boundary Condition ($y=0$) & Energy Quantization

* **Flagged Step:**
> *"$Y(y)=C_{y}Ai(z(y))$ where $C_{y}$ is a normalization constant and $z(y)=(\frac{2m^{2}g}{\hbar^{2}})^{1/3}(y-\frac{E_{y}}{mg})$ ."*
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Boundary Condition / Physical Completeness.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. The potential barrier is infinite at $y < 0$ ($V(y<0) = \infty$). Wave function continuity requires the ground boundary condition:



$$Y(0) = 0 \implies C_y Ai(z(0)) = 0$$


2. For non-trivial solutions ($C_y \neq 0$), $z(0)$ must equal one of the negative zeros $-a_n$ ($n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$) of the Airy function ($Ai(-a_n) = 0$, where $a_n > 0$):

$$z(0) = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} \left(0 - \frac{E_y}{mg}\right) = -\left(\frac{2}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} E_y = -a_n$$


3. Solving for the vertical energy eigenvalues $E_{y,n}$:

$$E_{y,n} = a_n \left(\frac{m g^2 \hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3}$$


4. Omitting $Y(0) = 0$ leaves $E_y$ continuous and the spatial state unconstrained at $y = 0$.




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$Y_n(y) = C_y Ai\left( \alpha y - a_n \right) \quad \text{for } y \ge 0, \quad \text{where } \alpha = \left(\frac{2m^2g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} \text{ and } Ai(-a_n) = 0$$
