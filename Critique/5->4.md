#### **Flaw 1: Algebraic Error and Dimensional Breakdown in $z(0)$**

* **Flagged Step:**
> "$z(0)=-\left(\frac{2m^{2}g}{\hbar^{2}}\cdot\frac{E_{y}^{3}}{m^{3}g^{3}}\right)^{1/3}=-\left(\frac{2mE_{y}^{3}}{\hbar^{2}g^{2}}\right)^{1/3}$"
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic (Fraction Simplification) and Dimensional.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. *Algebraic Proof:* Simplifying the mass $m$ and acceleration $g$ terms inside the root:

$$\frac{2 m^2 g}{\hbar^2} \cdot \frac{E_y^3}{m^3 g^3} = \frac{2 E_y^3}{\hbar^2} \cdot \left(\frac{m^2}{m^3}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{g}{g^3}\right) = \frac{2 E_y^3}{\hbar^2} \cdot \left(\frac{1}{m}\right) \cdot \left(\frac{1}{g^2}\right) = \frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}$$



Agent 4 incorrectly simplified $\frac{m^2}{m^3}$ as $m$ in the numerator instead of $m$ in the denominator.


2. *Dimensional Proof:* $z$ must be dimensionless ($\text{Dim}[z] = 1$). Evaluating the dimensions of Agent 4's result:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\left(\frac{2 m E_y^3}{\hbar^2 g^2}\right)^{1/3}\right] = \left( \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M}^3 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6})}{(\text{M}^2 \text{L}^4 \text{T}^{-2}) \cdot (\text{L}^2 \text{T}^{-4})} \right)^{1/3} = (\text{M}^2)^{1/3} = \text{M}^{2/3} \neq 1$$



Evaluating the correct formulation:

$$\text{Dim}\left[\left(\frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}\right] = \left( \frac{\text{M}^3 \text{L}^6 \text{T}^{-6}}{\text{M} \cdot (\text{L}^2 \text{T}^{-4}) \cdot (\text{M}^2 \text{L}^4 \text{T}^{-2})} \right)^{1/3} = 1$$




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$z(0) = -\left(\frac{2 E_y^3}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3}$$
