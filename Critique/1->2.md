### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### Flaw 1: Flawed Wavenumber Definition & Algebraic Forcing

* **Flagged Step:**
> *"where $k_x \equiv \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar}$ is the horizontal wavenumber."*
> 
> 
> *" $k_x = \frac{2m(\frac{1}{2}m u^2 \cos^2\theta)}{\hbar} = \frac{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$ "*
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic, Dimensional, and Logical (False Equality).
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. Substituting $X(x) = e^{i k_x x}$ into $\frac{d^2 X}{dx^2} + k_x^2 X = 0$ gives:

$$\frac{d^2}{dx^2}\left(e^{i k_x x}\right) + k_x^2 e^{i k_x x} = -k_x^2 e^{i k_x x} + k_x^2 e^{i k_x x} = 0$$



For this to satisfy $-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2X}{dx^2} = E_x X$, we must have:

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m} (-k_x^2) = E_x \implies k_x^2 = \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar^2} \implies k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar}$$



Agent 2 wrote $k_x = \frac{2m E_x}{\hbar}$, missing both the square root and the square on $\hbar$.


2. Dimensional Analysis check:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2m E_x}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2})}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{M T}^{-1} \neq \text{L}^{-1}$$


3. In Section 3, Agent 2 wrote $\frac{m^2 u^2 \cos^2\theta}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$, which is algebraically false unless $m u \cos\theta = 1$.




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar} = \frac{\sqrt{2m \left(\frac{1}{2}m u^2 \cos^2\theta\right)}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$


$$\text{Dim}[k_x] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot \text{L T}^{-1}}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{L}^{-1} \quad \text{(Dimensionally Correct)}$$

#### Flaw 2: Dirac Delta Normalization Constant Arithmetic Error

* **Flagged Step:**
> *"$\vert{}A\vert{}^2 \cdot 2\pi \delta(k_x - k_x') = \delta(k_x - k_x') \implies \vert{}A\vert{} = \frac{1}{2\pi} \implies X(x) = \frac{1}{2\pi} e^{i k_x x}$"*
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic / Arithmetic Error.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
From $2\pi \vert{}A\vert{}^2 \delta(k_x - k_x') = 1 \cdot \delta(k_x - k_x')$, matching coefficients gives:

$$2\pi \vert{}A\vert{}^2 = 1 \implies \vert{}A\vert{}^2 = \frac{1}{2\pi} \implies \vert{}A\vert{} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}}$$



Agent 2 omitted the square root operation on $2\pi$.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{i k_x x} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{i \left(\frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}\right) x}$$

#### Flaw 3: Box Normalization Constant Arithmetic & Dimensional Error

* **Flagged Step:**
> *"$\vert{}A\vert{}^2 \int_0^L dx = \vert{}A\vert{}^2 L = 1 \implies A = \frac{1}{L} \implies X(x) = \frac{1}{L} e^{i\left(\frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}\right)x}$"*
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic & Dimensional Error.
* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
1. Solving $\vert{}A\vert{}^2 L = 1$ for $A$:

$$\vert{}A\vert{}^2 = \frac{1}{L} \implies \vert{}A\vert{} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}}$$



Agent 2 wrote $A = \frac{1}{L}$.


2. Dimensional Analysis check:
If $X(x) = \frac{1}{L} e^{i k_x x}$, then $\int_0^L \vert{}X(x)\vert{}^2 dx = \int_0^L \frac{1}{L^2} dx = \frac{L}{L^2} = \frac{1}{L} \neq 1$.
Furthermore, for a 1D wave function, $[X(x)] = \text{L}^{-1/2}$, whereas $\frac{1}{L}$ has dimension $\text{L}^{-1}$.




* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$X(x) = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}} e^{i k_x x} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{L}} e^{i \left(\frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}\right) x}$$
