### 2. Flaws & Proven Counter-Derivations

#### **Flaw 1: Incorrect Intermediate Definition of Horizontal Wavenumber $k_x$**

* **Flagged Step:**
> `where $k_x = \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar} = \frac{mu\cos\theta}{\hbar}$`
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Algebraic and Dimensional.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
Starting from the decoupled horizontal equation:

$$-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} = E_x X(x) \implies \frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + \left(\frac{2mE_x}{\hbar^2}\right) X(x) = 0$$



Comparing this to the canonical harmonic oscillator equation $\frac{d^2 X(x)}{dx^2} + k_x^2 X(x) = 0$, the square of the wavenumber is:

$$k_x^2 = \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar^2} \implies k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2mE_x}}{\hbar}$$



Agent 1 wrote $k_x = \frac{2mE_x}{\hbar}$ (missing the square root on $2mE_x$ and missing the square on $\hbar$). Dimensional evaluation confirms:



$$\text{Dim}\left[\frac{2mE_x}{\hbar}\right] = \frac{\text{M} \cdot (\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-2})}{\text{M L}^2 \text{T}^{-1}} = \text{M T}^{-1} \neq \text{L}^{-1}$$



Substituting $E_x = \frac{1}{2}m u^2 \cos^2\theta$ into the correct square-root expression recovers the intended result:

$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m \left(\frac{1}{2}m u^2 \cos^2\theta\right)}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$k_x = \frac{\sqrt{2m E_x}}{\hbar} = \frac{m u \cos\theta}{\hbar}$$

#### **Flaw 2: Unconstrained Continuous Assignment of $E_y$ vs. Hard-Wall Boundary Condition $Y(0) = 0$**

* **Flagged Step:**
> `where $E_y = \frac{1}{2}m(u\sin\theta)^2$`
> 
> 


* **Nature of Error:** Physical and Boundary Condition.


* **Rigorous Mathematical Proof of Error:**
In Section 1, Agent 1 mandates the boundary condition $Y(0) = 0$ due to the infinite potential step at $y < 0$. The general bounded solution for $y \ge 0$ is $Y(y) = C_y \text{Ai}(z(y))$ with dimensionless parameter:



$$z(y) = \left(\frac{2m^2 g}{\hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} \left(y - \frac{E_y}{mg}\right)$$



Enforcing $Y(0) = 0$ requires:

$$Y(0) = C_y \text{Ai}\left(-\left(\frac{2}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} E_y\right) = 0$$



Since $\text{Ai}(x) = 0$ only at discrete negative zeros $-a_n$ (where $a_n > 0$ for $n = 1, 2, 3, \dots$), the vertical energy $E_y$ cannot be an arbitrary continuous classical parameter $\frac{1}{2}m(u\sin\theta)^2$; it is strictly quantized:



$$-\left(\frac{2}{m g^2 \hbar^2}\right)^{1/3} E_y = -a_n \implies E_{y,n} = \left(\frac{m g^2 \hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3} a_n$$



Setting $E_y$ directly to $\frac{1}{2}m(u\sin\theta)^2$ without noting vertical energy quantization violates the condition $Y(0) = 0$ unless $E_y$ happens to equal an eigenvalue $E_{y,n}$.


* **Corrected Formulation:**

$$E_{y,n} = \left(\frac{m g^2 \hbar^2}{2}\right)^{1/3} a_n \quad (n = 1, 2, 3, \dots)$$



*(Note: For macroscopic projectile parameters where $n \gg 1$, the quantum state corresponds to a high quantum number state or localized wavepacket centered around $E_{y,n} \approx \frac{1}{2}m(u\sin\theta)^2$.)*
